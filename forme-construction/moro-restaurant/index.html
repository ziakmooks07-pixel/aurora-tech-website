Our story

A neighbourhood restaurant with a little more character.

MORO is built around the simple pleasure of eating together. Our kitchen takes familiar ingredients and gives them a modern point of view, with open-fire cooking at the heart of the menu.

The room is warm, relaxed and designed for long dinners, celebrations and conversations that run past dessert.
