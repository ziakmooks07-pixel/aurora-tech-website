06 / FAQ

Questions, answered.

How long does a website take?

Most standard projects take around 2–4 weeks from kickoff to launch, depending on scope, content and feedback speed.

Do you provide the content?

Yes. We can work with your existing copy and images, or help structure and refine the content needed for the site.

Can you redesign my existing website?

Absolutely. We can refresh the visual design, improve the user journey, rebuild the site, or tackle specific pages and problem areas.

Do you offer ongoing support?

Yes. Ongoing updates, improvements and maintenance can be arranged after launch depending on what your business needs.

How do we get started?

Get in touch, tell us what you're building and what you want the website to achieve. We'll recommend the most suitable approach and scope.
